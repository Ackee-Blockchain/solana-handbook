## What is Solana?

Solana is a **high-performance blockchain platform** built to support 
<strong class="tooltip def-dapps">decentralized applications (dApps)</strong>, 
<strong class="tooltip def-cryptocurrencies">cryptocurrencies</strong>, 
and Web3 innovation. Created in 2017 by Anatoly Yakovenko and launched in 2020, Solana has quickly grown into one of the most active blockchain ecosystems.

Known for its **speed and low fees**, Solana stands out as one of the fastest-growing blockchains in Web3. While Bitcoin and Ethereum are limited in their transaction processing speed, Solana can handle thousands of transactions per second while keeping fees typically under a penny. This makes it attractive for developers building everything from 
<strong class="tooltip def-defi">decentralized finance (DeFi)</strong> apps to 
<strong class="tooltip def-nfts">non-fungible tokens (NFTs)</strong> and Web3 social platforms.

## The blockchain trilemma

Every blockchain faces a challenge known as the **blockchain trilemma**. This is the difficulty of achieving three goals at once:

**Scalability:** 

* Being able to efficiently process thousands of transactions per second.
* Measured in transactions per second (TPS).

**Security:**

* Ensuring the network is resistant to hacks and attacks.
* Protected through 
  <strong class="tooltip def-cryptography">cryptography</strong> and 
  <strong class="tooltip def-consensus-protocols">consensus protocols.</strong>

**Decentralization:**

* Preventing control by any single group or person. 
* Achieved by distributing power to a network of 
  <strong class="tooltip def-validators">validators.</strong> 

Most blockchains can achieve two of these but struggle with the third. For example, Bitcoin is highly secure and decentralized but processes transactions slowly. Some newer chains achieve speed but sacrifice decentralization.

**Solana's value proposition** is solving this trilemma. With technologies like 
<strong class="tooltip def-poh">Proof of History (PoH)</strong>, 
<strong class="tooltip def-bft">Tower BFT consensus</strong>, and 
<strong class="tooltip def-parallel-processing">parallel transaction processing</strong>, Solana aims to combine scalability, security, and decentralization without sacrificing one for the others. 

## Development on Solana

Solana is built with developers in mind. Its smart contracts are primarily written in **Rust**, a programming language known for speed and memory safety.

Developers can also use:

* **Anchor Framework**: A Rust-based framework that simplifies Solana development.
* **C and C++**: Useful for low-level control or porting existing code.
* **JavaScript/TypeScript <strong class="tooltip def-sdks">SDKs</strong>**: For building front-end applications that interact with Solana programs.

Beyond languages, Solana offers **robust tooling, libraries, and a strong developer community**, making it easier to build and launch decentralized applications.
